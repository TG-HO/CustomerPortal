Taj Gasoline Retail Station Manual

Standard Operating Procedures, HSSE Guidelines & Forecourt Service Excellence

1. Health, Safety & Environment (HSSE)

Every forecourt staff member must wear mandatory anti-static cotton uniforms and steel-toe safety footwear. Smoking, naked flames, and mobile phone usage are strictly prohibited across all dispenser islands and decantation zones.

2. 7-Step Service Excellence

  1. Warm Greeting: Guide customer vehicle and greet cordially.
  2. Fuel Confirmation: Confirm product (HSD, PMG, HOBC) and required volume/amount.
  3. Zero Meter Check: Verify and announce zero dispenser meter.
  4. Safe Dispensing: Secure nozzle in neck, refuel attentively.
  5. Value Added Services: Offer windshield wipe and oil level check.
  6. Receipt & Cash: State total volume and price, hand over print receipt.
  7. Courteous Farewell: Thank the customer and guide vehicle back onto road.

3. Tank Decantation Procedure

Connect static bonding earthing wire before opening any lorry hatch. Verify delivery dip with brass dip rod and water-finding paste. Ensure tank ullage capacity before starting discharge. Keep fire extinguishers within 5 meters.
